### A13  ·  Intermediate  ·  10 marks  ·  DESCRIPTIVE  ·  CO3
**Question:** A bloc of 3 countries forms an FTA then a CU. If external tariffs were 10%, 20% and 30%, why is the CET political?

**Expected Key Points:**
- An FTA lets each keep 10/20/30 on outsiders.
- A CU needs one CET, so someone must raise or lower protection.
- The 10% country fears trade diversion if CET is 30%; the 30% country fears import competition if CET is 10%.
- Revenue sharing also must be agreed.
- That is why CUs are harder than FTAs.

### A14  ·  Intermediate  ·  10 marks  ·  DESCRIPTIVE  ·  CO1
**Question:** What is trade creation versus trade diversion (Viner) in a customs union?

**Expected Key Points:**
- Creation: a member’s inefficient production is replaced by a more efficient partner.
- Diversion: a cheaper outsider is replaced by a costlier partner because of the CET.
- Welfare is ambiguous.
- Exam answers should not praise every FTA as efficiency.
- Rules of origin in FTAs create a related distortion.
