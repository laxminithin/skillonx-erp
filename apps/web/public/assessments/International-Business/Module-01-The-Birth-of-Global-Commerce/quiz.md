### Q01  ·  Easy
**Question:** FDI differs from trade mainly because FDI:

- **A.** Forbids any goods movement
- **B.** Places controlling investment inside the host country
- **C.** Never involves management
- **D.** Is only tourism

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** FDI is control-seeking investment, not only shipping goods.
