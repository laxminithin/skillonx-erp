**Question:** Define international business and contrast it with domestic business on any four dimensions.

### A07  ·  Intermediate  ·  10 marks  ·  DESCRIPTIVE  ·  CO2
**Question:** Explain the Uppsala internationalization process and psychic distance.

**Expected Key Points:**
- Johanson and Vahlne argue that firms learn from current operations and raise commitment stepwise.
- They start in psychically close markets with agents or exports.
- Psychic distance is perceived differences in language, culture and institutions, not mere kilometres.
- Production subsidiaries come after sales knowledge accumulates.
- The model is a learning theory, not a law that no firm can skip stages.
