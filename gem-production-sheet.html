From panels to a finished clip

  1. Split the grid. Run python3 slice_storyboard.py storyboard.png (in your Survey folder) to export panels/panel_01…12.png. Regenerate any panel at higher resolution if it looks soft — start frames should be crisp.
  2. Set the character reference. In Kling use "Elements" / face reference; in Runway Gen-4 use a reference image. Feed the same girl's face every shot so she never drifts.
  3. Generate shot by shot. Upload each panel as the start frame, paste its motion prompt + the style suffix, render 2–3 takes at low motion strength, keep the calmest.
  4. Assemble in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (both free). Order 1→12, trim to the beat, add gentle cross-dissolves between shots 4–5 and 8–9.
  5. Voice & music. Record or AI-generate a soft narrator for panels 1–8 and the grandmother's three lines (9–11). Lay a warm score under it; drop the music out on the "shhh" in shot 11.
  6. Title & end card. Open on the series title, close on shot 12 with a "To be continued…" — the untold secret is your season-long hook.

One thing to watch: AI video can't render your speech-bubble text, and on-frame text usually comes out garbled — so keep panels 9–11 text-free and add every word as narration/subtitles in the edit. That also lets you dub the series into other languages later without regenerating a single shot.
